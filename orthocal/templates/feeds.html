RSS

Orthocal.info provides an RSS feed that can be used to embed the daily readings on your website or used in an RSS feed reader. The default is the Slavic tradition, Gregorian calendar, but the Julian calendar and the Greek tradition (Antiochian, Greek Archdiocese; beta) are also available
